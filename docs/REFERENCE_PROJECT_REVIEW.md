# Henry’s Farm Adventures review

Reviewed 13 November 2026.

The official Space Apps winner listing identifies Zumorroda-X, Cairo, as the 2025 Art & Technology global winner. The project’s own submission describes a journey with Henry through heatwaves in India, floods in Pakistan, salinization in Egypt, drought in the United States and crop management in Brazil.

The submission connects a farmer’s problem to NASA information and a task. Pakistan uses before/after satellite imagery and placing straw or drainage. Other examples include a heat quiz and crop-condition matching. It lists JavaScript/Phaser, Python and Piskel, and discloses AI assistance.

The live demo loaded its pixel-art Farming Crisis introduction and Henry dialogue. This review did not complete every country or verify all scientific claims in the competitor’s implementation. Its submission is participant-authored.

## What NEILO can apply

- Put the goal in a character’s request. The crew asks for a crop plan or an evidence report.
- Make a source visible in the action. The RAD graph uses actual archive values and the crop cards show approximate source ranges.
- Make each chapter’s action distinct. Routing power, growing a tray and inspecting evidence should feel different.
- Present a clear effect before the explanation. The crop waits, a relay stays queued, or a report gains evidence.
- Show these actions and sources in the submission video. A polished explanation page alone does not demonstrate gameplay.

These are design inferences from the project. An award listing does not disclose the jury’s detailed causal reasoning. NEILO’s direction remains the child’s personal explorer, Neilo’s memory and linked decisions.
