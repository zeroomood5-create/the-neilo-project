# New report delta

The newly supplied TXT report contains the prior radiation/crop material plus two proposed health activities and a two-bar health/energy HUD.

NASA’s bone-changes page supports teaching that spaceflight can change bone health and that countermeasures require sustained research and management. The cardiovascular page concerns microgravity fluid shifts and possible difficulties on return to gravity. Both concern a defined spaceflight context. The NEILO child is currently inside a fictional Mars base. A later crew-recording or orbital training scene can explain the context.

Suggested use is a short crew briefing about exercise or a timeline showing orbit and return, with a follow-up question and citation. A one-click health recovery or a personal medical score cannot be derived from these pages. The two bars in the report are proposed game-design quantities and were not approved as a replacement for the current base power/battery/water interface.

No new numerical health mechanic is introduced by importing this report. Current implemented expansion uses CSA crop examples, the archived Mars RAD sample and the M12 paper.
