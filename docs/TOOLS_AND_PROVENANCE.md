## AI disclosure for submission

NEILO was developed with AI assistance. Codex assisted with JavaScript implementation, design iteration, debugging, scientific-source screening, documentation and tests. Image generation was used for illustrative environments and character art. The team supplied generated cinematic clips and ElevenLabs voice recordings. Dynamic speech uses browser speech synthesis. Scientific measurements are attributed to their original agencies and study authors. Game resource units, simulated calendars and robot turns are authored model values. Team review of scientific interpretation and user testing remain necessary.

## Scope of validation
