References: Mission control equipment and exterior base palette.
Start frame: medium close view of a sealed battery cabinet with a simple clear window showing a readable illustrated battery cutaway, in the same warm-and-teal Mars base style.
End frame: matching composition, cabinet softly illuminated, connected line continuing out of frame toward the pump.

Prompt:
Create an educational cinematic insert for NEILO, a polished stylized 3D Mars adventure for children aged seven to twelve. Match the attached room reference exactly in material language and lighting. Begin outside a small base window looking at a solar panel under sunlight. Make a slow, continuous camera move along a visible cable through a sealed wall connection and finish at a battery cabinet. Use a restrained warm luminous pulse along the cable as an illustrative energy-flow overlay. Treat this glow as a teaching visualization, not literal particles inside metal. In a brief stylized cutaway, show energy being stored symbolically within the battery, without pretending to depict detailed chemistry. Keep the wiring connected and spatially readable. The exterior stays outside a sealed habitat. End on the cabinet and its outgoing cable. Gentle purposeful movement, no flashing, no unexplained machinery, no people or text. Preserve the architecture and palette. No claim of a particular voltage, capacity, efficiency or NASA-certified equipment.

Narration pairing: water-science-1 and water-science-2. These may need separate edits or continuation takes depending on final recorded duration.
